PolishPal / Brand exploration / Phase 01

Three ways to say cześć.

Original, flat symbols built for a warm, free Polish-learning site. Each is drawn as a single ink, with generous cuts and circle-safe padding. Choose a direction before we replace the site’s assets.

CONCEPT A / OUR PICK

Hello bloom

A folded-paper tulip: three crown cuts, two generous leaves, and a central stem. A compact nod to Polish wycinanki, with no national symbols.

The wide crown and open cuts survive at 16px; the leaf pair gives it a recognisable base. Recommended for its connection to the site’s folk motif.

CONCEPT B

The friendly Ł

A custom, soft-ended Ł built from a sturdy upright and a rising diagonal. Polish orthography becomes the whole symbol, rather than a detail inside an illustration.

The diagonal remains a clear two-pixel stroke at 16px. The simplest silhouette of the three, though it signals “Polish” more strongly to people who already know Ł.

CONCEPT C

A little back-and-forth

Two broad quotation cuts face each other across a shared space. Their rounded shoulders suggest two equal partners taking turns: the learner and their pal.

Both halves remain separate at 16px, with two open, square counters. More abstract than A or B, and less specifically Polish; the alternating tails carry the personality.
