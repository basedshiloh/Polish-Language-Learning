Rig contract

  • Each rotating part declares data-pivot="x y" in SVG user units. Core pivots are identical across all poses.
  • data-rig="character" contains the complete puppet; the shadow is its sibling. Hop this wrapper, keeping the shadow on the ground.
  • data-part="head" owns both eyes, both lids, and the mouth. Tilt it to move the whole face together.
  • Eyes expose data-state="open|happy"; lids have their own groups. Change visibility, without scaling to zero.
  • The mouth exposes exactly one visible closed, open-s or open-l group at a time. Timing here is illustrative, not audio lip-sync.
  • The prop pivot follows the item’s grip or board anchor. The manifest records its per-pose value.
