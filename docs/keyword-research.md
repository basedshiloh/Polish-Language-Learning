## 2026-07-11 — Pass 2: Culture / mentality / pop-culture focus (fresh angle)

Deliberately steered away from grammar/pronunciation/app-comparison territory
(already covered in Pass 1) since the user has seen that flavor of suggestion
repeatedly across sessions. This pass mines Polish *mentality*, folklore,
science history, and modern subculture — categories (`memes-pop-culture`,
`arts`) that are thin on the site relative to `culture` and `vocabulary`.

### 6 Circles Content Plan

**Primary Keyword: "polish superstitions"**
- Culture.pl has the dominant existing article ("13 Super-Polish Superstitions");
  real search demand, medium competition, but no PolishPal coverage and it
  naturally links to the existing `polish-folklore-creatures` post.

**Sub-topic A — Death, remembrance & solemn ritual**
1. Zaduszki: Poland's Night of a Million Candles (All Souls' Day, Nov 2) — `zaduszki` — seasonal spike late Oct/early Nov; strong visual hook (millions of grave candles)
2. Polish Funeral Customs and Mourning Etiquette — `polish funeral customs` — thin competition, practical for expats/heritage Poles
3. Dziady (Forefathers' Eve): The Pagan Ritual Behind Mickiewicz's Most Famous Poem — `dziady poland` — niche literary/folklore crossover, almost no blog-native coverage, ties `arts` + `culture` categories together

**Sub-topic B — National mentality & humor**
4. Polish Complaining Culture: Why Narzekanie Is Basically a National Sport — `polish complaining culture` — validated real interest (YouTube videos, Quora threads) but only thin blog coverage exists; highly shareable
5. Polish Directness: Why Poles Skip Small Talk and What to Expect — `polish directness culture` — pairs well with expat-focused posts (`best-cities-in-poland-for-expats`, `cost-of-living-in-poland`)
6. Polish Gallows Humor: Why Poles Joke About the Worst Things — `polish dark humor` — unique angle, very low direct competition, pairs with `polaczek-cebulaczek`

**Sub-topic C — Science, street food & modern subculture**
7. Polish Inventions That Changed the World: Czochralski, the Bulletproof Vest, and More — `polish inventions` — the Czochralski method story (accidental 1916 lab discovery, underpins 90%+ of modern semiconductors) is a strong hook; existing competitors (jwp-poland, Battery Empire) are dry listicles, room for a narrative version
8. Zapiekanka: Poland's Cold War-Era Answer to Pizza — `zapiekanka` — completes the street-food cluster alongside `what-is-pierogi`, `what-is-paczki`, `what-is-oscypek`; strong Gierek-era backstory hook
9. Poland's Counter-Strike Dynasty: How a Small Country Dominated Esports — `polish esports counter strike` — "Golden Five" (TaZ, NEO, pashaBiceps, Snax, byali) is a genuinely great underdog story; the `memes-pop-culture` category currently has zero gaming content despite otherwise covering Witcher, disco polo, Żabka memes
